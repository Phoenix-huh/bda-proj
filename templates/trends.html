Trends: 2020 vs Later Years

Longitudinal AQI comparison across India: evaluating the 2020 COVID-19 lockdown window against 2021–2025 post-lockdown rebound trajectories using a fixed balanced panel of 58 cities.

⚠️ Methodological Notes & Caveats:

AQI only; 2025 data end 31 Oct 2025; fixed panel of 58 cities; 2022 window was the highest (cause not analysed); not comparable with the 2015-2020 results in /covid.

  • AQI only: The post-2020 dataset reports daily Air Quality Index values per city; individual gas concentrations are not present in this release.
  • Data range: 1 Jan 2020 through 31 Oct 2025.
  • Fixed panel of 58 cities: To eliminate geographic composition bias as the national monitoring network expanded from ~139 cities (2020) to ~287 cities (2025), analysis is restricted to the 58 cities with ≥80% data completeness in the 25 Mar – 31 May window every single year.
  • 2022 window was the highest (cause not analysed): The 2022 lockdown window experienced the highest median AQI (138.5, +58.5% over 2020).
  • Not directly comparable: Sources use different station sets and are not directly comparable with the 2020 lockdown results in /covid. In addition, these CPCB-derived AQI values are kept in a separate SQLite store (daily_aqi) and not mixed with the 2015–2020 station-level gas models, as AQI calculation methodologies can differ.
